# Data ethics & content licensing

## Consent & privacy
- Pastors consent to AI analysis of check-ins, in plain language, in their language; can opt out of AI analysis and still use check-ins.
- Pastors can see their own encouragement text; risk labels are admin-only and framed as prompts for care, not judgement.
- Right to export and delete personal data.
- L2 responses anonymous by design.
- Before any real pilot: review with the partner church leadership; document who sees what.

## AI limits (communicate honestly)
The check-in analyst suggests; a human pastor-leader decides. It is not counselling or diagnosis.

## Content licensing — important
- Many modern Bible translations (e.g. NIV, ESV) are copyrighted. Use public-domain translations (KJV, WEB, ASV)
  or obtain a licence. Same for Gita and Dhammapada translations — prefer public-domain editions.
- Record `translation` and `license` on every `source_chunk`.
- Have at least one knowledgeable reviewer per tradition check the 30 seed entries.
